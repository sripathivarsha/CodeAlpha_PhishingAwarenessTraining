## Overview
This project is a cybersecurity awareness presentation focused on phishing attacks and social engineering.

The presentation explains how to:
- Recognize phishing emails
- Identify fake websites
- Understand common social engineering techniques
- Recognize phishing red flags
- Follow safe cybersecurity practices
- Respond after interacting with a phishing link
- Test phishing awareness through an interactive quiz

## Topics Covered
1. What Is Phishing?
2. Types of Phishing
3. Recognizing Phishing Emails
4. Identifying Fake Websites
5. Social Engineering
6. Real-World Phishing Example
7. Phishing Prevention Best Practices
8. Interactive Quiz
9. Response After Clicking a Phishing Link

## Real-World Example
The presentation includes a documented phishing case reported by Meta involving more than 39,000 fake websites impersonating Facebook, Messenger, Instagram, and WhatsApp login pages.

Source:
Meta Newsroom – Taking Legal Action Against Phishing Attacks
